The screen as your avatar

His screen is the avatar. It sits at home in the intro, docks in a corner once you scroll, and can be dragged anywhere. Its expressions dissolve into each other on the LED grid.

Homepage

Hover the word Pip or the email links. Click Pip, drag him, throw him. Scroll: he hops into the left margin and sits beside the work index (hover a project and he moves to it and plays a tiny loop of what it does), grows into a full-width display for "Available for hire" (click it to copy the email), and docks in the footer as a Bangalore clock before resting. Drop him anywhere; he goes back to his job as soon as you scroll. Turn sound on in the footer if it is off.

One screen (experiment)

Everything on one screen, no scrolling. Pip is a tall LED panel beside the column and shows whatever you point at: each project turns him its colour with his reaction, "Now" runs Available for hire, Email shows COPY. Click him to poke him: a ripple runs out from where you click. His dots behave like real LEDs: they fade slowly, glow, and travel to make each new face.

One screen, with wind (experiment)

A centred column and a light breeze of leaves. Point at something and the breeze slowly turns toward it. Leaves settle gently on the ground and pile up. The page's light follows Bangalore's day: gold around sunrise, pale by day, warm toward sunset. Click a project (Lumen, Smart GIF, Atlas) and it opens in place, in the same column, growing from where you clicked: a demo at the top, details below, × to close. Footer: stillness (or Space) stops time for a moment; sound is a soft wind made in the page, swelling with each gust.
